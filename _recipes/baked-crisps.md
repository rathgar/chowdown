ingredients:
  - - Beremeal
    - ¾ cup Beremeal Flour	 
    - ¾ cup Strong white bread flour
    - ½ tsp baking powder
    - 2 tbsp Olive oil
    - ½ cup Cold water
  - - Chickpea
    - 1 cup Chickpea flour
    - ½ cup Fine polenta
    - ½ tsp Baking powder
    - ½ cup Warm water
    - 1 tbsp Olive oil
  - - Spelt
    - 1½ cups Spelt flour
    - ¼ cup Fine Semolina
    - ½ tsp Salt
    - 2 tbsp Olive oil 
    - ½ cup Cold water

directions:
  - In the large bowl, mix together the dry ingredients including any flavourings. Stir in the grated Parmesan cheese if you are using it.
  - Add the olive oil and cold water. Mix and knead briefly until the dough comes together. The cheese may make the dough feel slightly "short" (tender/crumbly), but the strong flour should keep it cohesive. Add water very slowly if it won't hold together.
  - Roll out (must be thin!), brush lightly with olive oil, and score once it's on the baking tray.
  - Bake at 190°C and check them after 10 minutes.

### Flavour Variations



#### Thyme, Garlic & Lemon Zest

This combination is clean, aromatic, and allows the Beremeal's nuttiness to shine.

- 1 tablespoon fresh Thyme leaves (or 1 tsp dried Thyme)
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
​- Zest of ½ lemon (Adds a bright, savoury lift)
- ¼ tsp Black Pepper

#### Cumin & Thyme

  - 1 tbsp dried thyme
  - 1 tsp whole or ground cumin

#### Smoked Paprika & Onion

- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder + 
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
