ingredients:
  - - Binding syrup
    - ½ cup honey
    - ¼ cup brown rice syrup (a natural alternative to glucose syrup)
    - 1 tablespoon coconut oil (optional, for a smoother texture and flavor)
  - - Mix
    - 1½ cups shredded coconut (unsweetened)
    - 1 cup crisped rice cereal
    - ½ cup cashews, roughly chopped
    - ½ cup dried apricots, chopped
    - ½ cup dried cranberries (or any other dried fruit you prefer)
    - ¼ cup dried apple pieces, chopped
    - 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
    - 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  - - Coating
  - ½ cup good quality dark chocolate (at least 70% cocoa), chopped
  - 1 teaspoon coconut oil (optional, to help it melt smoothly)


directions:
  - Prepare the dry ingredients. In a large bowl, combine the shredded coconut, crisped rice, cashews, chopped apricots, cranberries, and apple. Mix well to ensure an even distribution. In a separate small bowl, mix the cocoa powder and ground ginger.
  - Make the binding syrup. In a small saucepan over low heat, combine the honey, brown rice syrup, and optional coconut oil. Stir until the mixture is smooth and fully combined. Be careful not to let it boil vigorously, as this can make the syrup too hard.
  - Combine and press. Pour the hot syrup over the dry mix. Using a sturdy spatula, fold everything together until all the dry ingredients are evenly coated. This step requires quick work as the syrup will start to cool and thicken.
  - Form the bars. Line an 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper, leaving some overhang to act as handles. Pour the mixture into the pan and press it down firmly and evenly using the back of the spatula or a piece of parchment paper. The firmer you press, the more cohesive the bars will be.
  - Refrigerate. Place the pan in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours, or until the mixture is completely firm. This allows the binders to set and the bars to hold their shape when cut.
  - Cut and coat. Once the mixture is firm, lift it out of the pan using the parchment paper handles. Place it on a cutting board and cut into 12-16 bars.
  - Melt the chocolate. In a double boiler or a microwave, gently melt the chopped dark chocolate with the optional teaspoon of coconut oil, stirring until smooth. Dip one side of each bar into the melted chocolate.
  - Final set. Place the chocolate-coated bars on a clean piece of parchment paper. Let the chocolate set completely at room temperature or in the refrigerator for a few minutes before storing. Store the finished bars in an airtight container at room temperature.
